# Third-party material

Sekai Stickers is CC0-1.0, like the rest of this repository, except:

- `js/data/characters.ts` is the sticker list from
  [TheOriginalAyaka/sekai-stickers](https://github.com/TheOriginalAyaka/sekai-stickers)
  (`src/characters.json`), under the MIT License; see `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/sekai-stickers-MIT.txt`.
- The character pictures are Project Sekai artwork (SEGA / Colorful Palette). They are not in this
  repository; the plugin downloads them from st.ayaka.one when you first use a character.
- `assets/fonts/YurukaStd.otf` and `assets/fonts/ShangShouFangTangTi.ttf` are the fonts the web maker
  uses, converted from its WOFF2 files. They belong to their respective owners.
